# Privacy Policy for Minesweeper Decision Assistant

Last updated: 2024-XX-XX (請填入今天的日期)

This privacy policy describes how **Minesweeper Decision Assistant** handles data.

### 1. Data Collection
We do **NOT** collect, store, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, or user-identifiable data. This extension is designed to run entirely within your local browser environment.

### 2. Permissions Usage
To provide its core functionality, this extension requires the following permissions:
- **`activeTab` & `scripting`**: Used only to read the game board data on `minesweeper.online` and display probability hints.
- **`storage`**: Used only to save your UI preferences (e.g., display settings) locally on your device.

### 3. Data Transmission
No data is ever sent to external servers. All calculations for minesweeper probabilities are performed locally on your computer.

### 4. Third-Party Sharing
Since we do not collect any data, no information is shared with, sold to, or used by any third-party entities.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page.
